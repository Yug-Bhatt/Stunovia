# Stunovia

### AI-Powered Personalized Student Intelligence Platform

Stunovia is an AI-powered student platform designed to reduce information overload by bringing relevant technology news, internships, and career opportunities into one personalized dashboard.

The platform uses a student's interests, skills, and career goals to provide more relevant information and opportunities.

---

## Project Overview

Engineering students often have to search multiple platforms to find:

- Technology news
- Internship opportunities
- Job opportunities
- Industry updates
- Relevant career opportunities

Stunovia aims to solve this problem by providing a centralized platform where students can discover information relevant to their academic and career interests.
